**Papa Sherpa privacy policy** · effective 2026-10-16

Papa Sherpa is the Papa Squad's own bot. Papa Squad Staff run it. It reads the server so it can point members to the places that suit them.

**Your consent**
Sherpa is on for everyone by default. You can withdraw that consent at any time and give it again later. Use `/privacy`:
• `opt-out approach`: Sherpa won't approach you on its own.
• `opt-out processing`: Sherpa stops keeping your messages and sending them to its AI model. `/find` is off for you while this is set.
• `opt-in approach` or `opt-in processing`: switches either back on.
• `backfill`: after opting back in, Sherpa re-reads your messages from the last 6 days within about six hours. Older messages can't be recovered.
• `settings`: shows your current choices.
• `delete-my-data`: erases the messages Sherpa has kept from you and turns processing off.

**What Sherpa holds**
• Places: the server's channels, forums and posts, with short summaries. Staff can withhold or exclude any of them. A Post is described by its opening message. Opting out or deleting your data does not yet remove that: delete the Post in Discord, or ask Staff to exclude it.
• Recent messages: their text and author, including the author's Discord username and display name, used to summarise what each place has been talking about lately. Names are kept only while Sherpa holds a message of yours, and only to keep them out of what goes to the AI model. Summaries describe places, not people.
• Your `/find` question: sent to the model to answer you, then dropped. It isn't stored or logged.
• Your choices in `/privacy`.
• If you opt out of processing: your username, display name and server nickname, only so they're kept out of what other members' messages send to the AI model. Removed when you opt back in or delete your data, and from backups within 30 days.

**How long it's kept**
• Raw message text: at most 7 days.
• Profiles: Sherpa keeps none today. If it ever does, a profile holds at most 30 interactions or 12 months, whichever is shorter. It ends when you leave the server.
• Erasure: `delete-my-data` takes effect at once; if the file is busy, it's wiped within six hours. A message, post or channel deleted in Discord leaves the public index within one sync (every six hours), and Sherpa's kept messages within 7 days. Either way it leaves backups within 30 days.
• Activity summaries: kept with who contributed to them, so a deletion can find them, and removed within 15 days of the place's newest message when it was summarised.
• Staff edit history: kept for 90 days, and removed at once on erasure.
• Your opt-out is kept even after erasure, because it's what honours your request.

**Who sees it**
• Discord, which carries every message.
• Railway, which hosts the bot and its backups.
• OpenRouter, which forwards model requests. Only providers that keep no data and don't train on it are used.
• Cloudflare R2, when it starts holding backups.

They process data only to provide their service to us. We don't share your information with any associated entity: no partner, sponsor or other community. **We will never sell, rent or trade any information you share.**

**Public history**
This project's code is public. Catalog data committed to its repository before 2026-10-14 stays in its public history. It names and summarises places, and includes the opening message of each Post. Nothing has been committed since that date.

**Children**
Discord requires members to be at least 13. If you believe Sherpa holds data from someone younger, tell Staff, and we'll erase it.

**Questions**
Ask any Staff member. If this policy changes, `/privacy policy` shows the new version and its date.
